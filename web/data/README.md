# web/data

`mandi_prices.csv` holds cleaned daily wholesale (mandi) prices from June 2024 onwards for Tomato, Onion, Potato, Rice, Broken rice and Groundnut in Karnataka and Maharashtra.
- **Loaded into:** the MySQL table `mandi_prices`, by `scripts/mysql/load-mandi-prices.mjs`.
- **Used for:** the price suggestions.
- **Generated by:** `ml/export_serving_data.py` (see `ml/README.md`).

**Source:** Agmarknet, Directorate of Marketing & Inspection, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, via [data.gov.in](https://data.gov.in/resource/variety-wise-daily-market-prices-data-commodity).

**Licence:** [Government Open Data License – India](https://data.gov.in/government-open-data-license-india).

**Changes from the source:**
- rows dropped by the cleaning rules in `ml/README.md`
- prices converted from ₹/quintal to ₹/kg
- market names normalised (`market_key`)
- varieties grouped (`variety_group`)
